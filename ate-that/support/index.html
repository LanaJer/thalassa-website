Ate That

Support

Ate That helps you log approximate calories by voice. Hold the Action Button, describe what you ate, hold again to submit, and review the result in the app.

For help, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, contact ops@thalassa.dev.

Getting Started

Open Ate That and complete onboarding. The app can use its included OpenAI key for testing, and you can optionally add your own OpenAI API key in Settings.

Use the microphone button in the app for your first meal, then add Ate That to Control Center and the Action Button for faster logging.

Action Button Setup

Add the Ate That control to Control Center, then choose that control in iPhone Action Button settings. After setup, you can start and submit a recording without unlocking the phone.

The app shows recording, transcribing, estimating, and saved states through Live Activities and Dynamic Island when available.

Apple Health Export

Apple Health export is optional. Enable it in Ate That Settings if you want saved meals to write estimated dietary calories to Apple Health.

Ate That does not read Apple Health data. Edited or deleted meals attempt to update or remove Health samples created by Ate That.

Common Issues

If a recording does not save, check that the device has network access and that the recording includes a clear meal description.

Empty or unclear recordings are intentionally ignored so Ate That does not invent a meal.

Privacy

Ate That stores meal records locally on your device. Audio and transcripts are sent directly to OpenAI only when you submit a meal for transcription and calorie estimation.

Read the full Ate That Privacy Policy.
