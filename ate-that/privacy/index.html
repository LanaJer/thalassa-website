Privacy Policy

Ate That keeps your meal history on your device. Voice recordings and meal details are processed only when needed to provide transcription and nutrition estimates.

Effective: August 24, 2026

Who We Are

Ate That is provided by Thalassa Softwaraki LTD, a company registered in Cyprus as HE 466541 (VAT 60106502M), based in Limassol, Cyprus. Thalassa Softwaraki LTD is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

This policy applies to the Ate That iOS app and the online services used by the app.

Meal History on Your Device

Your meal history is stored locally on your device using Apple's storage frameworks. It may include meal titles, estimated calories and nutrients, item breakdowns, timestamps, transcripts, prompt versions, and Apple Health export status.

Audio memos for pending or failed entries may also remain on your device so that you can replay, retry, replace, or delete them. We do not keep a server-side copy of your meal history or these saved memos.

Your Account

An Ate That account is created when you use Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us a developer-specific identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address. The address may be an Apple private relay address. We do not receive your Apple Account password.

We store an internal account ID, the Apple identifier, the email information Apple provides, encrypted Apple authorization credentials, and sign-in session records. The app stores its refresh token in the iOS Keychain. See Sign in with Apple & Privacy for information about Apple's processing.

Voice and Meal Processing

When you submit a voice entry, the app sends the recording over an encrypted connection to our backend. The backend sends the audio to OpenAI for transcription, then sends the transcript and relevant meal context to OpenAI to estimate or edit calories, macronutrients, alcohol, and meal items.

We do not write your submitted audio, transcript, meal context, or inference result to its database, object storage, or application logs. This content may be held briefly in memory or temporary instance storage while the request is processed, and is discarded when the request finishes. If a request fails, any review memo shown in Ate That is the local copy on your device.

OpenAI processes this content as our service provider. Ate That requests that text responses are not stored as application state. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in, but content may be retained in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless a different data-control arrangement applies. Learn more in OpenAI's API data controls.

Service and Usage Data

To operate and protect the service, we keep limited metadata associated with your account. This may include a request ID, operation type, model and prompt version, token counts, response status, processing time, app version, build number, and timestamps. This metadata does not include your recording, transcript, meal names, ingredient list, or inference result.

Our infrastructure providers may also process technical information such as IP address, network and device information, and service logs for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. We use inference usage records to enforce rate limits, investigate failures, prevent misuse, and understand service costs.

Apple Health

Apple Health export is optional and requires your permission. If enabled, Ate That writes its estimates for dietary energy, protein, fat, carbohydrates, and alcohol to Apple Health. Ate That does not import your general Apple Health history into the app. It may access samples created by Ate That in order to reconcile, replace, or delete them when you sync, edit, or delete a meal.

Health data is handled through Apple's HealthKit framework and is not sent to our servers or OpenAI. You can change Ate That's Health access in iOS Settings or the Health app.

How We Use Data and Our Legal Bases

We process personal data only as needed to:

  • create and secure your account and provide transcription, estimation, editing, and syncing features;
  • authenticate requests, maintain sessions, and respond to support requests;
  • protect the service, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and manage capacity and costs; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Where EU or EEA law applies, we rely on performance of our contract with you to provide the service, our legitimate interests in operating and securing it, your consent for optional Apple Health access, and legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw Health permission at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Service Providers and International Processing

We use the following providers to operate Ate That:

  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, App Store distribution, and diagnostics you choose to share;
  • Google Cloud for backend compute, database hosting, backups, networking, and operational logging; and
  • OpenAI for audio transcription and meal estimation or editing.

These providers may process data outside Cyprus or the EEA. Where required, we rely on contractual and other lawful safeguards for international transfers. Provider processing is also governed by their terms and privacy notices, including the Google Cloud Privacy Notice and OpenAI's API data controls linked above.

We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or disclose it to advertisers.

Retention

Local meals and review memos remain on your device until you delete them, delete all local data, or remove the app. Account and usage metadata is generally retained while your account is active. Active app sessions expire after 30 days and may be replaced or revoked sooner.

Submitted meal content is not retained by us after the inference request ends. Infrastructure logs and OpenAI's processing may have separate limited retention as described in this policy and the linked provider notices.

When you delete your account, its account, session, and usage records are deleted from the active database. Residual copies may remain in protected rotating database backups until they are overwritten; we currently retain seven backup copies. We may retain information longer where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the data we process. These include encrypted network connections, encrypted Apple credentials, hashed app refresh tokens, restricted production access, and managed cloud security controls. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Analytics and Tracking

Ate That does not include third-party advertising or behavioural analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may provide standard App Store, TestFlight, performance, and crash diagnostic information according to your Apple settings.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. To make a request, email ops@thalassa.dev. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Cyprus, this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual meals or all local meal data from Ate That. If Apple Health export is enabled, Ate That also attempts to remove the Health samples it created.

You can delete your Ate That account from Settings. Account deletion removes your server-side account, sessions, and usage metadata as described under Retention, but it does not automatically erase meal history stored locally on your device. Use the separate local data deletion control if you want to remove that history as well.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Ate That changes. We will post the revised policy here, update its effective date, and provide additional notice in the app when a change is material.
