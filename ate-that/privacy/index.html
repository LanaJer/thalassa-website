Ate That

Privacy Policy

Ate That is a local-first calorie tracking app. It is designed to help you log approximate calories while keeping your meal history on your device.

Last updated: May 30, 2026

What Ate That Stores

Ate That stores your meal records locally on your device using Apple's local storage frameworks. Meal records may include meal titles, estimated calories, nutrients, item breakdowns, timestamps, transcripts, and Apple Health export status.

Ate That does not create user accounts and does not operate a customer database for your meal history.

OpenAI Processing

When you submit a voice recording, Ate That sends the audio directly to OpenAI to create a transcript. Ate That then sends the transcript or meal text directly to OpenAI to estimate calories, nutrients, and meal items.

The app may use an included OpenAI API key or an API key you provide in Settings. A key you provide is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Apple Health

Apple Health export is optional. If you enable it, Ate That writes estimated dietary calories to Apple Health for meals logged in the app.

Ate That does not read your Apple Health data. If you edit or delete a meal, Ate That attempts to update or remove the Apple Health sample it created.

Analytics and Tracking

Ate That does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Thalassa Softwaraki LTD does not sell your data.

Apple may provide standard TestFlight, App Store, and crash diagnostic information according to your Apple settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual meals in Ate That. The app also provides a local data deletion control for removing meals stored by Ate That on your device.

If Apple Health export is enabled, deleting meals also attempts to remove Apple Health calorie samples created by Ate That.
