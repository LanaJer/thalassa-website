Products

Purpose-built modules for support operations

Each product runs independently or as a connected platform — start with one channel and scale into a full AI support stack.

Customer Support AI for Text Chats

Automate high-volume conversations with a guardrailed assistant that understands context, follows policy, and escalates edge cases to human agents — resolving repetitive intents instantly while staying on brand and on policy.

  • Safety guardrails that prevent risky or off-policy answers.
  • CRM-native workflows with Intercom, Salesforce, and Freshdesk.
  • Conversation summaries and action extraction for faster follow-up.

RCal for macOS

A private, local calendar bridge for keeping selected work events visible in a destination calendar you control.

  • Native EventKit access—no calendar credentials.
  • Full details, title-only, or Busy blocks.
  • Learn about RCal →

Voice Support Module

AI-assisted support over telephone lines and web calls: triage incoming requests, answer common issues, and route callers to the right specialist with context attached.

  • PSTN telephony and browser-based voice sessions.
  • Real-time transcription, intent detection, and call summaries.
  • Agent-assist prompts and post-call CRM updates.

RAG Module with Access Controls

Semantic answers across internal knowledge, policies, and support history — with access boundaries so responses respect per-employee and per-group permissions.

  • Semantic search across documents, tickets, runbooks, and SOPs.
  • Permission-aware retrieval by employee and organizational group.
  • Traceable citations and audit-friendly response logs.

Enterprise On-Premises Deployment

Run the platform in your own infrastructure when data residency, governance, or internal security policy requires full environment control — private networking, isolated model access, and enterprise identity standards with feature parity to cloud deployments.

  • Private cloud or data-center deployment options.
  • Enterprise SSO and internal security tooling integration.
  • Designed for strict compliance and regulated industries.
